THE STARTING POINT

A Qwen backbone.

The proposed pretrained path starts with dense Qwen3-8B. Selected feed-forward layers would be upcycled into compatible experts, sharing embeddings and attention.

TWO DIFFERENT ROLES

Workers ≠ neural experts.

Mushrooms organize missions and review work. Neural experts are feed-forward blocks inside the model. A full chatbot is not one of these experts.

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Does specialization help?

Train new capacity and its router while initially preserving the backbone. Measure the whole model: more experts and lower loss do not guarantee better results.

03 / A NETWORK THAT CAN ADAPT

Computers leave.
The right copy stays.

In the community vision, different machines host different experts. If a machine drops out, a replica of the same expert version can take over without changing the computation.

MVP2 proves this across local processes. A secure, practical wide-area network is still a research goal.

04 / THE RESEARCH, AS IT STANDS

Small proofs.
Clear boundaries.

Real neural execution, deliberately tiny. These experiments test the mechanisms, not the promise of useful intelligence at scale.

EXECUTED ON CPU

Train. Reload.
Route. Recover.

Randomly initialized models of roughly 39–48K parameters demonstrate expert/router training, exact checkpoint reload, real tensor offload and same-version replica recovery.

A MEANINGFUL REJECTION

Lower loss.
Still not good enough.

The generated-task candidate failed fresh output checks despite lower training loss. It was rejected and the baseline retained. The gate did its job.

STILL TO BE ESTABLISHED

Pretrained quality.
Real-world scale.

No full pretrained 8B run, useful capability gain, WAN deployment or distributed training has been validated. Offloading tensors is not proof of lower total memory use.

18 October 2026 · Standard suite: 1,102 passed, 71 explicit skips. Optional actual-CPU MoE suite: 193 passed, zero skips. These are software and mechanism checks, not intelligence scores.

A separate synthetic two-cycle experiment demonstrated promotion, rejection and rollback. Beneficial pruning has not been measured.

What the next proof must show

A pretrained experiment on available hardware, followed by repeatable gains on useful held-out missions without unacceptable regressions, latency or resource costs.

Network privacy, malicious contributions, trustworthy independent review and practical hardware fit remain open. Hidden states are not a privacy guarantee; different local reviewer identities are not adversarial independence.
